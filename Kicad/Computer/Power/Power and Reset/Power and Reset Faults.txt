Power and Reset - schematic faults
==================================

From the socket review of 2026-11-23.  The board's own circuit hasn't been reviewed.

1. The socket symbol has the two resets swapped (pins 29 and 32)

   On this board pin 29 (J3.9) is ~{Reset}_{5V}, from IC4 (74LVC1G17 on 5V), and pin 32 (J3.12) is ~{Reset}_{3.3V},
   from IC7 (74LVC1G17 on 3.3V).  The HCP65:Power_and_Reset symbol that host boards place names pin 29 ~RES_3V and pin
   32 ~RES_5V.

   Effect: hosts that want the 3.3V reset get the 5V one.  The Pico GPIO Controller Board and the Pico SDRAM board both
   drive the Pico's RUN pin from pin 29, which can damage the Pico (the RP2040 isn't 5V tolerant).

   Fix: swap the two pin names in the symbol and update the host boards.  See their faults files.
