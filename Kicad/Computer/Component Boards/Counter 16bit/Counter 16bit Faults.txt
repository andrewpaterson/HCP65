HCP65 Counter 16bit - schematic faults
======================================

From the review of 2026-11-23.

What the board does: four 74LVC161s (IC1 C0-C3, IC2 C4-C7, IC3 C8-C11, IC4 C12-C15) make a synchronous 16 bit
counter on CLK, cleared by ~{Reset} on ~MR.  Latch Low and Latch High, inverted by IC6 (74LVC2G04), drive ~PE of the
low pair (IC1, IC2) and the high pair (IC3, IC4), so each byte is loaded from D0-D7 on a rising CLK while its strobe
is high.  The upper stages take TC_{0..3} on CEP and the stage below's TC on CET.  TC_{0..3} and TC_{12..15} are on
the socket (pins 19 and 20).  No host board uses this board at present.

1. The upper 12 bits count whether or not Count Enable is high (IC1)

   The same fault as the Counter 24bit's fault 1.  IC1 has Count Enable on CEP (pin 7) and CET (pin 10) tied to
   3.3V.  The 74LVC161's TC is high when CET is high and the counter is at $F, whatever CEP is, so TC_{0..3} is high
   whenever C0-C3 is $F, and IC2-IC4 (CEP = TC_{0..3}) count on every clock then.

   Effect: with Count Enable low the counter isn't held.  Whenever the low nibble is $F the upper 12 bits go up by
   one each clock.  TC_{0..3} on pin 19 is also high while the count is stopped.

   Fix: put Count Enable on IC1's CET (pin 10) too.

2. TC_{12..15} ignores the low nibble (IC2 pin 10)

   IC2's CET (pin 10) is tied to 3.3V, so the CET chain IC2 -> IC3 -> IC4 starts at C4.  TC_{12..15} (IC4 pin 15,
   socket pin 20) is high whenever C4-C15 is $FFF, whatever C0-C3 and Count Enable are.

   Effect: TC_{12..15} is high for 16 clocks, not one, at the top of the count, so a second counter cascaded from it
   (its Count Enable or CET on TC_{12..15}) counts 16 times, not once.

   Fix: feed IC2's CET from TC_{0..3} in place of 3.3V (after fault 1's fix TC_{0..3} carries Count Enable too), so
   that TC_{12..15} is high only at $FFFF while counting.  The CET chain is then four stages, about 4 x 7.5ns
   worst case, well inside a clock.
