HCP65 Clock Hold Standard - schematic faults
============================================

1. IC3 is a NOR gate where the hold code's output enables need an OR

   IC3 (74LVC2G02, dual NOR) makes the nets ~{RAM}+~{RD} (1Y, pin 7, from
   ~{Device RAM} and ~{RD}) and ~{RAM}+~{WD} (2Y, pin 3, from ~{Device RAM} and
   ~{WD}).  They drive IC2's (74LVC244) active low output enables, ~{1OE}
   (pin 1) and ~{2OE} (pin 19).  The nets are named as ORs, and an OR is what
   the enables need: each half of IC2 is meant to drive the hold code only
   during a device RAM read, or a device RAM write.

   With a NOR, an output is high only when both its inputs are low, so each
   half of IC2 is disabled during the one cycle it is for and enabled during
   every other cycle.

   Effect: on every register and ROM cycle (and when the device isn't
   selected) both halves of IC2 drive H0-H2 together, against each other
   (their inputs differ: device RAM read 001, write 000) and against IC5,
   which drives the register and ROM codes.  During device RAM cycles nothing
   but the pull-ups drives them.  The hold code is never what it should be.

   The Clock Hold Select board makes the same two nets with a 74LVC32 (OR).

   Fix: IC3 a 74LVC2G32 (dual OR), same pinout.

   Every board with a Clock Hold Standard has this: Audio Board, Blitter
   Board, Coprocessor Board - Main, Graphics Board, LCD Board, Default Board
   (Main), PS2 Board, SD Card Board, UART Board and Video Board - Signals.

Found in the review of 2026-11-23
---------------------------------

2. Possible: one decoupling capacitor short on 3.3V

   There are three ICs on 3.3V (IC2, IC3, IC5) but only two 1uF capacitors (C1, C2) beside C11 (10uF) at the
   regulator.  IC4 (5V) has C4.  There is no C3 on the sheet, which looks like the missing one.

   Effect: one of the 244s has no local decoupling; with both halves switching three lines at once that adds
   ground bounce on the hold code.

   Fix: add C3, 1uF from 3.3V to GND beside whichever of IC2 or IC5 has none.
