HCP65 GPIO 4bit - schematic faults
==================================

From the review of 2026-11-23.

What the board does: IC1 (74VHCT573, 5V) latches the data bus on Write GD: D0-D3 are the four pin values (GD0-3) and
D4-D7 their output enables (OE0-3).  IC6 (74AHCT132) NANDs each OE with Enable to drive the active low ~{OE}s of IC4
(74AHC125, 5V), so a pin is driven only when its OE bit and Enable are both high; that polarity is right.  IC2
(74LVC573, 3.3V) reads GPIO0-3 back onto D0-D3 (D4-D7 read 0) while Read GD is high, through IC3 (74LVC1G04).  The
bit order through the board is consistent.  Used on the PS2 Board and the SD Card Board.

1. Possible: the pins are driven at 5V, which the SD Card Board passes to the card (IC4)

   IC4 runs from 5V, so a driven high is 5V.  On the SD Card Board GPIO0-3 are SD CLK, SD CMD, DAT0 and DAT3, which
   reach the card through the SD Card Full Socket's CBT3244 bus switch.  That switch is powered from 5V, so it
   doesn't clamp a high to 3.3V; the card is a 3.3V device with its pull-ups to 3.3V.

   Effect: the SD card's inputs see well above its 3.6V maximum, which can damage the card or latch it up.  (On the
   PS2 Board the 5V level is right, as long as the driver only ever drives low and uses the OE bits to release the
   open collector lines.)

   Fix: for the SD Card Board, run IC4 from 3.3V (the 74AHC125 works from 2V and its inputs take 5.5V at any VCC),
   for example with a supply jumper or a 3.3V build of this board; or power the CBT3244 so it limits the swing to
   3.3V.
