HCP65 Clock Doubler - schematic faults
======================================

From the review of 2026-11-23.

What the board does: Fast CLK (J1.5) is squared up by IC4 (74LVC1G17) into Clean Fast CLK.  R1 (750R) and C1 (16pF)
delay it, and IC2 (MAX9010 comparator) compares the delayed copy on IN- with 1.65V from R2/R3 on IN+, so IC2's output
is the delayed clock inverted.  IC3 (74LVC2G86) XORs Clean Fast CLK with it to give 2x Fast CLK (J1.20).  No host
board uses this board yet.

1. The comparator runs from 3.3V (IC2)

   IC2's VCC (pins 5, 6) is the board's 3.3V.  The MAX9010 datasheet gives a supply range of 4.5V to 5.5V, and an
   input common mode range of -0.2V to VCC - 1.9V, which at 3.3V is 1.4V, below the 1.65V reference on IN+.

   Effect: IC2 is outside its supply range and its inputs are outside the common mode range at the switching point,
   so the delayed clock, and with it 2x Fast CLK, may be missing, late or erratic.

   Fix: power IC2 from 5V (J1.1) with its own decoupling capacitor.  Its TTL output (2.4V minimum high) is fine into
   IC3's 5V tolerant input.  IN- still swings to 3.3V, a little above 5V - 1.9V; the datasheet says the output
   doesn't reverse when overdriven, so that is acceptable, or drop the RC's swing with a divider.

2. Possible: the doubled clock's duty cycle depends on the input frequency

   Because IC2 inverts, 2x Fast CLK is XNOR of the clock and its delayed copy: it falls at every edge of Fast CLK
   and rises again one delay later.  The delay is about 0.69 x R1 x C1 (8ns) plus IC2's propagation delay (5ns
   typical, 9ns maximum), so roughly 13ns to 17ns, and it changes with the parts' tolerances and temperature.

   Effect: the low time is fixed at about 13ns to 17ns and the high time is whatever is left of each half period.
   At 25MHz in (20ns half period) the output is high for only 3ns to 7ns; above about 30MHz in the high pulse
   becomes a runt or vanishes.  Any difference between IC2's rising and falling delays, or a Fast CLK duty cycle
   other than 50%, makes alternate output periods unequal.

   Fix: size R1 and C1 for a delay of about a quarter of Fast CLK's period, so the output is near 50%, and state
   on the sheet which input frequency the board is for.
