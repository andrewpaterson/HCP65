HCP65 Counter 16bit with IO - schematic faults
==============================================

From the review of 2026-11-23.

What the board does: four 74LVC161s (IC7 C0-C3, IC8 C4-C7, IC9 C8-C11, IC10 C12-C15) make a synchronous 16 bit
counter on CLK, cleared by ~{Reset} on ~MR and loaded from the start address on a rising CLK while ~{Load Count
Start} is low.  The start address is held in two 74LVC573s, IC2 (low) and IC1 (high, wired bit reversed but
consistently), transparent while Write Start Address Low/High is high, so they latch at the end of the write.  The
count is read back through two 74LVC574s, IC11 and IC12, clocked together by Latch Address = AND(Latch Address
Enable, Read Address Low) (IC3) and enabled by the inverted Read Address Low/High (IC4); they only drive the bus
during their read.  Used on the Audio Board (B1), Blitter Board (B5) and Graphics Board (B5).

1. The upper 12 bits count whether or not Count Enable is high (IC7)

   The same fault as the Counter 24bit's fault 1.  IC7 has Count Enable on CEP (pin 7) and CET (pin 10) tied to
   3.3V, so TC_{0..3} is high whenever C0-C3 is $F, and IC8-IC10 (CEP = TC_{0..3}) count on every clock then.

   Effect: with Count Enable low the upper 12 bits still go up by one each clock while the low nibble is $F.  No
   harm on the current hosts: the Audio Board ties Count Enable high, and on the Blitter and Graphics Boards the
   clock only runs while counting (see their Faults files).

   Fix: put Count Enable on IC7's CET (pin 10) too.

2. TC_{12..15} ignores the low nibble (IC8 pin 10)

   IC8's CET (pin 10) is tied to 3.3V, so TC_{12..15} (IC10 pin 15, socket pin 40) is high whenever C4-C15 is $FFF,
   whatever C0-C3 and Count Enable are.  No host uses pin 40 at present.

   Effect: a counter cascaded from TC_{12..15} would count 16 times at the top of this one's count, not once.

   Fix: feed IC8's CET from TC_{0..3} in place of 3.3V (with fault 1's fix), so that TC_{12..15} is high only at
   $FFFF while counting.

3. Possible: the read-back snapshot races the counter (IC3, IC11, IC12)

   IC11 and IC12 are clocked by IC3's AND of Latch Address Enable and Read Address Low, with nothing to keep that
   edge away from CLK.  On the Blitter and Graphics Boards Latch Address Enable is CLK1, the counter's own clock, so
   during a low read every CLK1 rising edge clocks the counter and, one AND gate later (about 1-4ns), the snapshot.
   The 74LVC161's Qn change 1.5-7.3ns after CP, and the 74LVC574 needs its data held 1.5ns after its CP, so the
   snapshot is taken while the count is changing.  On the Audio Board (Latch Address Enable = Read Address Low) the
   snapshot is taken on the read's rising edge, asynchronous to CLK, and can land on a count edge the same way.

   Effect: now and then a read gives a mix of the old and new count, e.g. $0F -> $10 read as $1F or $00.  With
   Latch Address Enable = CLK1 the low byte on the bus can also change during the read itself, on each CLK1 edge.

   Fix: take the snapshot on the other edge of the counter's clock, e.g. Latch Address = AND(~CLK, Read Address
   Low), or hold the counter still while it is read.
