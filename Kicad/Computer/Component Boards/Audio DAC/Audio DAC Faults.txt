Audio DAC - schematic faults
============================

From the symbol library review of 2026-11-23.  The rest of this board hasn't been reviewed.

1. The stereo jack's left and right channels are swapped

   The symbol CUI_Devices:SJ1-3525NG-GR names pin 2 R, 3 L, 4 RN and 5 LN.  The Same Sky (CUI) datasheet has pin 2 as
   the tip, 3 the ring, 4 the tip switch and 5 the ring switch, and the tip is left by convention.

   Effect: the channel this board means as left comes out on the ring (right).  Nothing is damaged, and each switch
   pin still goes with its own channel.

   Fix: rename the symbol's pins 2 = L, 3 = R, 4 = LN, 5 = RN (see Symbols/Symbol Faults.txt, item 4), update the
   symbol here and re-route, or swap the two channels where they reach the jack.

Found in the review of 2026-11-23
---------------------------------

What the board does: IC6 (MAX5102, dual 8 bit DAC at 5V) takes D0-D7 and A0 on ~{WR}, with a 2.06V reference from
R1/R2.  Each output is AC coupled (C5, C12, 100uF, with 1M to GND), filtered by three 100R/0.1uF RC sections, and
buffered by IC7 (AS358A) running from 5V and the -5V that IC1 (TC7660) makes, then through 540R to the jack.

2. The TC7660's LV pin is grounded at 5V (IC1 pin 6)

   The TC7660 datasheet says LV should be connected to GND only for supplies below 3.5V and otherwise left to
   float; its absolute maximum ratings limit the current into LV to 20uA above 3.5V.  Here V+ is 5V and LV is on
   GND.

   Effect: the converter is used outside its ratings; it may draw excess current through LV, run its oscillator
   wrongly or latch up, losing the -5V rail for IC7.

   Fix: leave pin 6 unconnected.

Checked and not a fault: IC6 pin 3 (SHDN) is active high ("Connect SHDN to GND for normal operation"; a high
shuts the DACs down, from the local MAX5102 datasheet), so driving it from ~{Enable} is right.
