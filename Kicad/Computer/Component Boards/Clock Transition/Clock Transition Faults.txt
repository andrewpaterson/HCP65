HCP65 Clock Transition - schematic faults
=========================================

From the review of 2026-11-23.

What the board does: it switches Clock (J1.11) between Fast Clock (J1.5) and Slow Clock (J1.2) under ~{Select Fast}
(J1.9).  The fast enable is NOR(~{Select Fast}, Clock2_Q), synchronised by two flip-flops (IC4, 74LVC74) clocked by
Fast Clock into Clock1_Q; the slow enable is AND(~{Clock1}_Q, ~{Select Fast}), synchronised by IC7 clocked by Slow
Clock into Clock2_Q.  Each enable waits for the other clock to be off, and Clock = OR(Fast AND Clock1_Q, Slow AND
Clock2_Q) (IC5, IC1).  ~{Reset} clears all four flip-flops.  The cross-coupled break-before-make is right.  It is
used by the Speed project.

1. The enables change while their own clock is high, so the output gets runt pulses (IC4B, IC7B, IC5A, IC5B)

   The 74LVC74 is rising edge triggered, so Clock1_Q (IC4 pin 9) changes a clock-to-Q delay after Fast Clock rises,
   while Fast Clock is high, and it is ANDed with that same Fast Clock in IC5A.  The same is true of Clock2_Q (IC7
   pin 9) and Slow Clock in IC5B.

   Effect: when a clock is switched off, its gate passes a high pulse only a clock-to-Q delay wide (a few ns) on
   the last rising edge: a runt pulse on Clock.  When a clock is switched on, its first high phase is shortened by
   the same delay.  A runt can clock some flip-flops and not others downstream.

   Fix: change each final enable while its clock is low: clock the second flip-flop of each pair (IC4B, IC7B) from
   the inverted clock (a 74LVC1G04 or the spare gate of a 74LVC2G04 on each clock), or keep the flip-flops and
   gate with the clock inverted (enable AND NOT clock, then invert the output), the usual glitch free clock mux.

2. Possible: no clock at all while ~{Reset} is low

   ~{Reset} clears all four flip-flops, so both enables are off and Clock is held low for the whole reset and for
   two cycles of the chosen clock after it.

   Effect: if Clock is PHI2 to a W65C816, the processor needs clock cycles while RESB is low to reset; with no
   clock during reset it may not start properly.

   Fix: if Clock must run during reset, preset the slow pair instead of clearing it (IC7 ~{SD} from ~{Reset},
   ~{RD} to 3.3V) so Slow Clock is selected during reset.
