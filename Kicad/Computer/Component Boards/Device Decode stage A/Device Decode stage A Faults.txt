Device Decode stage A - schematic faults
========================================

From the review of 2026-11-23.

What the board does: it splits a device's address space into ROM, registers and RAM, and makes the device's resets.
- IC2 (74LVC138) is enabled when A16, A15, A14 and A13 are all 0 (~E1 = A16, ~E2 = A15+A14 from IC6, E3 = ~A13 from
  IC13).  It decodes A10-A12: Y0 is ~{Device ROM} at $0000-$03FF and Y1 is ~{Device Registers} at $0400-$07FF.
- ~{Device RAM} is low for the rest of the space.  IC3 makes ~(A16+A15), ~(A14+A13) and ~(A12+A11), and IC5 (a
  3-input AND) is high only when A11-A16 are all 0.
- IC6 ORs each of the three with ~{Device Select}.
- IC4 ANDs the two reset inputs into ~{Reset}, and IC13 makes Reset, ~A3 and ~A4.

The board is used on the Audio, Blitter, Coprocessor Main, Graphics, LCD, PS2, SD Card, UART, VIA and Video Signals
boards.  Those with a 32KB device space tie A15 and A16 to GND.  As drawn the decode is right, and every part's
pinout is right except IC3's.

1. IC3's symbol has the 74LVC00's pinout, not the 74LVC02's

   The symbol 74LVC02APW,118 in Symbols/Nexperia.kicad_sym numbers its pins like a '00 (1A=1, 1B=2, 1Y=3, 2A=4,
   2B=5, 2Y=6, 3Y=8, 3A=9, 3B=10, 4Y=11, 4A=12, 4B=13).  The real 74LVC02A is 1Y=1, 1A=2, 1B=3, 2Y=4, 2A=5, 2B=6,
   3A=8, 3B=9, 3Y=10, 4A=11, 4B=12, 4Y=13.

   Effect, with the real part fitted:
   - Pin 4 (2Y) drives A16, pin 10 (3Y) drives A13 and pin 13 (4Y) drives A11.  These are the device's own address
     inputs, so on every board that carries this one, IC3 fights the device bus address lines.  On the boards that
     tie A16 to GND, pin 4 is shorted to GND.
   - Pin 1 (1Y) is shorted to GND, and its input pin 3 floats.
   - Pins 6, 8 and 11 are inputs where the gate outputs should be, so the ~(A16+A15), ~(A14+A13) and ~(A12+A11) nets
     float.  IC5's three inputs float, so ~{Device RAM} is random.

   Fix: correct the symbol's pin numbers (it is also used by Device Decode stage B, Processor Full Device, Kernal
   Mode, Device Reset, Instruction Code and MPU NMI) and re-route IC3.
