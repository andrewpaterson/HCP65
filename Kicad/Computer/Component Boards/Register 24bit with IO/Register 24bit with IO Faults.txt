Register 24bit with IO - schematic faults
=========================================

From the socket review of 2026-11-23.  The board's own circuit hasn't been reviewed.

1. The socket symbol has the six strobes one pin off (pins 21-27)

   On this board the strobes are Latch Bank, Latch High, Latch Low, Read Bank, Read High and Read Low on pins 22-27,
   and pin 21 isn't connected.  The HCP65:Register_24bit_with_IO symbol puts them on pins 21-26.  The data bus, the
   outputs and the power pins all match.

   Effect: on the Graphics Board (B13) every strobe reaches the wrong function.  The host's "Write Bank" goes nowhere,
   "Read Bank" opens the low latch, and this board's Read Low (IC6 pin 6) floats, so its read buffer turns on at
   random.  See Graphics Board Faults.txt, fault 4.

   Fix: move the six strobes to pins 22-27 in the symbol and update the Graphics Board.
