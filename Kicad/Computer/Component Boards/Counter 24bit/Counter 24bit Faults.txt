HCP65 Counter 24bit - schematic faults
======================================

1. The upper 20 bits count whether or not Count Enable is high

   IC3 (74LVC161, C0-C3) has Count Enable on CEP (pin 7) and CET (pin 10)
   tied to 3.3V.  Its TC (pin 15, TC_{0..3}) is the CEP of every other stage
   (IC4, IC6, IC7, IC9, IC10), whose CETs carry the TC of the stage below.
   The 74LVC161's TC is "HIGH when CET is HIGH and the counter is at terminal
   count": it doesn't depend on CEP.

   So while C0-C3 is $F, TC_{0..3} is high whatever Count Enable is, and the
   upper stages count on every clock.

   Effect: with Count Enable low the counter isn't held.  Whenever the low
   nibble is $F the upper 20 bits go up by one each clock, so the count jumps
   by 16 at a time.  On the Video Board's pixel address counter: a pixel
   pattern with a 0 in it (a pixel shown for more than one clock) skips 16
   bytes at every address ending in F, and a row start address ending in F
   runs away through the rest of horizontal blank.

   Fix: put Count Enable on IC3's CET (pin 10) too, so that TC_{0..3} is only
   high while counting.

   Every board with a Counter 24bit has this: Video Board - Memory, Blitter
   Board, Graphics Board and SD Card Board.
