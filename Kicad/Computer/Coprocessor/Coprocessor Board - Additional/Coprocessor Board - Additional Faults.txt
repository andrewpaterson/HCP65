Coprocessor Board - Additional - schematic faults
=================================================

From the symbol library review of 2026-11-23.  The rest of this board hasn't been reviewed.

1. The W65C816 Power boards get no ground (B1, B25)

   This schematic's copy of the HCP65:W65C816_Power symbol is out of date.  It has GND on pin 12 and pins 3 and 10
   N.C., so the board connects /GND only to pin 12 of B1 and B25.  The W65C816 Power board has its GND on J2 pins 3 and
   10, and its pin 12 isn't connected; the library symbol and the Processor Board (B21) agree with that.

   Effect: the LK112 regulator on each W65C816 Power board has no ground return, so W65C816V2 and W65C816V3 are
   undefined.  The processors they supply won't run, and they can be damaged.

   Fix: update the symbol from the library (Tools > Update Symbols from Library), which puts GND on pins 3 and 10,
   and re-route B1 and B25 on the PCB.

2. Minor: the W65C816_Latch_Time sockets' copies are also out of date (B30, B36)

   Pin 7 is N.C. in this schematic's copy and Presence in the library.  The board leaves it unconnected either way, so
   there is no effect, but updating the symbols removes the difference.


Found in the socket review of 2026-11-23
----------------------------------------

3. /3.3V has no source

   /3.3V only reaches the two Selector 12bit boards' A11/B11 inputs (B20, B26 pins 3 and 15) and the two W65C816 Latch
   boards' supply (B30, B36 pin 6); the Latch boards have no regulator of their own.  On Coprocessor Board - Main the
   Clock Hold Standard board (B4) supplies /3.3V, but this board has no such board, and none of J8-J13 carries 3.3V.

   Effect: the Latch boards are unpowered, so Bank Latch2 and Bank Latch3 are undefined.  Selector bit 11 floats, so
   the /Q2 and /Q3 presence bits (J12 to Coprocessor Board - Main J7, then B10 S10/S11) read at random.

   Fix: add a 3.3V source (for example an AP7365 from 5V, as the other boards use), or bring 3.3V over J8-J13.

4. The W65C816 Latch boards get PHI2 where they need ~PHI2 (B30, B36 pin 3)

   B30 pin 3 is /CPHI23 and B36 pin 3 is /CPHI22, the same nets as the W65C816 Minimals' PHI2 (B24, B18 pin 26), but
   the Latch board's pin 3 is ~PHI2.  The same fault as Coprocessor Board - Main Faults.txt fault 2, with the same
   effect: the bank latch keeps the data byte instead of the bank.

5. The Minimals' data transceivers are enabled for the whole cycle (B18, B24 pin 38; B30, B36 pin 9)

   The Latch boards' ~{Data Output} isn't connected, and the Minimals' ~DE is /CAccess2 and /CAccess3 directly.  The
   same fault as Coprocessor Board - Main Faults.txt fault 3: the 74LVC4245 fights the 65816's bank byte during PHI2
   low on every read.

The possible faults 4-6 in Coprocessor Board - Main Faults.txt (the transceiver, the Minimal's 74LVC4245 orientation,
now confirmed, and the random control state at power up) apply to this board's coprocessors too.


Found in the review of 2026-11-23
---------------------------------

What the board does: it carries coprocessors 2 and 3 (B18, B24) with their memory (B22, B28), selectors, Supports and
Clock board (B35), plugged onto Coprocessor Board - Main.  J8-J13 sit under the Main board's J6, J3, J5, J4, J7 and J2
and their pins line up one to one, on the PCB and in the schematic: 12V and the 40MHz clock come in on J10, CAccess2-3,
CRun2-3, C~{Reset}2-3 and ~{Reset} on J13, the main bus's AM0-AM18, DM0-DM7, ~{Main RD}, ~{Main WD} and M~{Memory4-7}
on J11, J8 and J9; INT2-3, ~{INT2-3} and the presence bits Q2-Q3 go back on J10 and J12.  IC3 (LD1117S50C) makes the
board's 5V from 12V.

6. The overclocked coprocessors' supply and timing (B35)

   B35 gets /40MHz from the Main board's oscillator over J10 pin 3 and passes it to both W65C816s undivided.  The
   overclock is intended; what follows from it (a 5.0V supply where ~5.3V is needed, the LK112s overheating, and
   12.5ns for a memory read) is the same as Coprocessor Board - Main Faults.txt fault 7.

The possible faults 9-11 and minor fault 13 in Coprocessor Board - Main Faults.txt apply to this board too: a write to
the Flags can pulse C~{Reset}2-3; stopping a coprocessor with PHI2 high leaves ~{WD} on across a hand-over; IC3 drops
7V for the board's 5V (two Memory 1MB, four Selectors, two Supports, the Clock and the Transceiver); and Q2-Q3 show
that B20 and B26 are fitted, not the W65C816s.  Fault 8 there (~{INT}2-3 floating) is what happens on the Main board
when this board isn't fitted.
