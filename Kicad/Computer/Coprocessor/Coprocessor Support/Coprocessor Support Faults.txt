Coprocessor Support - schematic faults
======================================

From the review of 2026-11-23.

What the board does: the schematic's note says "The coprocessor signals an interrupt by touching address 0F:FFFF".
IC5 and IC6 (74F30, 8-input NAND, 5V) decode CA0-CA7 and CA8-CA15 all high, IC7 (74LVC00) CA16.CA17 and CA18.CA19,
and IC1 (74LVC32) ORs the four into ~{CATOP}, low at $FFFFF (any bank $xF, as only four bank bits exist).
~{CATOP} drives IC2A's (74LVC74) ~SD, so INT and ~{INT} latch the request.  IC2A is cleared by Reset (IC3A) OR CLR
(Write Clear, an active-high write pulse from Device Decode stage B), through IC1D and IC3B.  IC4 (74LVC1GX04) makes
the two 512KB chip selects from CA19: Y (pin 6) = CA19 for Memory1 and the unbuffered X2 (pin 4) = ~CA19 for Memory0.
The pinouts agree with the datasheets, and the unused gate inputs are tied off.

1. The interrupt decode isn't qualified by the clock, so it can fire on a passing address (IC1, IC2A)

   ~{CATOP} is a purely combinational decode of the address bus and sets IC2A asynchronously, at any time in the
   cycle.  During PHI2 low the address is settling, and CA16-CA19 are the W65C816 Minimal's bank latch while it is
   transparent (its IC5 and IC20 pass D0-D3 straight through), so they show whatever is on D0-D3 until the bank byte
   is driven: at first the previous cycle's read data.  VDA and VPA aren't checked either.

   Effect: spurious coprocessor interrupts.  For example any read at $xx:FFFF that follows a read whose data has $F
   in its low four bits, such as the IRQ/BRK vector's high byte at $00:FFFF after its low byte, shows $FFFFF for a few
   nanoseconds; so can address changes like $xF:7FFF to $xF:8000, if the rising bits get there before the falling
   ones.  The LVC74's ~SD responds to pulses of a few ns.  (This comes on top of the hosts' Bank Latch phase fault,
   Coprocessor Board - Main Faults.txt fault 2, which makes CA16-CA19 the data byte.)

   Fix: only let ~{CATOP} reach ~SD during PHI2 high with VDA high: ~SD = ~{CATOP} OR ~PHI2 OR ~VDA (IC7's spare
   NAND can make ~(PHI2.VDA), and a third OR input is needed).  The address and the latched bank are stable by then.
   The connector has no spare pin, so PHI2 and VDA need two more pins, and the Minimal would have to bring VDA out.
