Mainboard Full - schematic faults
=================================

From the socket review of 2026-11-23: every socket's pins were compared with its daughter board.  The rest of the
board hasn't been reviewed.

1. MPU1 and MPU2 can't select main memory (B10, Main Memory Full Enable)

   B10 ORs the active-low ~Main lines into /~Main, which is ~E1 on the Main Memory Select boards (B1, B2, B3, B9).  Its
   inputs are MPU0's ~Main and its devices 3, 4, 5 and 16-19, and the MPU1 and MPU2 devices 16 and 17.  /MPU1 ~Main
   (MPU1.B9) and /MPU2 ~Main (MPU2.B9) go only to the Small Abort boards B5 and B11.

   Effect: when MPU1 or MPU2 reads or writes main memory and nothing else is asking for it, no memory chip is selected.

   Fix: put /MPU1 ~Main and /MPU2 ~Main on two of B10's spare inputs (pins 13, 14, 22 and 23 are tied to 3.3V).

Checked and not a fault: the abort boards only compare the ~Main lines within each MPU's slot group.  Two MPUs' raw
~Main lines may overlap by design, because only one MPU's slot is open at a time: the Triple MPU Clock Generator's two
3-stage rings (reset to one 0 each, and only ever rotated) open one ~Main Access at a time, and each Processor Board
gates its drive onto AM/DM with ~Main AND ~Main Slot.  This holds only if every device board in the MPU1/MPU2 slots also
gates its bus drive with ~Main Slot, which wasn't checked.

Not settled: B12 pins 10, 13 and 16 put the Triple MPU Clock Generator's ~MPU Clock0/1/2 onto nets named MPU0/1/2
CLK.  Whether that inversion is intended depends on the clock and slot phases.
