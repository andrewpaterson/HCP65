Processor Full Device - schematic faults
========================================

From the review of 2026-11-23.

What the board does: it is device 7 of MPU0 on the Mainboard Full (B13), and lets MPU0 interrupt, NMI and reset the
other MPUs and see their interrupts.
- IC4 (74LVC138) decodes A0-A2, enabled by ~{Device Select} with A4 low (~E2) and A3 low (E3 = ~A3 from IC10's
  fourth gate).  Y0 to Y3 are four registers.
- Writes: IC10's gates 1-3 make Y1.WD, Y2.WD and Y3.WD as the clocks of IC13 (resets), IC12 (NMIs) and IC14 (IRQs),
  three 74LVC273s.  D0-D2 reach IC12 and IC14 through IC17 (3G17), and IC13 inverted through IC15 (3G04).  All three
  are cleared by ~{Device Reset}, so after reset the other MPUs are held in reset, with no NMI or IRQ to them.
- The outputs go through IC1 and IC2 (3G04) to ~{MPUn NMI}_{out} and ~{MPUn IRQ}_{out}.  The reset outputs go
  straight to ~{MPUn Reset}_{out}.
- Reads: IC9, IC8, IC7 and IC11 (74LCX540, inverting) put the IRQ inputs, reset outputs, NMI outputs and IRQ outputs
  onto DO0-DO2 for Y0-Y3.  IC16 (74LVC541) puts DO0-DO2 (and 0s on D3-D7) onto D0-D7.
- ~{Device Interrupt} = AND of the three ~{MPUn IRQ}_{in} (IC5).

The Mainboard Full's B13 socket matches this board's J1 pin for pin.  Every part's pinout is right except IC10's.

1. IC10's symbol has the 74LVC00's pinout, not the 74LVC02's

   The symbol 74LVC02APW,118 in Symbols/Nexperia.kicad_sym numbers its pins like a '00 (1A=1, 1B=2, 1Y=3, 2A=4,
   2B=5, 2Y=6, 3Y=8, 3A=9, 3B=10, 4Y=11, 4A=12, 4B=13).  The real 74LVC02A is 1Y=1, 1A=2, 1B=3, 2Y=4, 2A=5, 2B=6,
   3A=8, 3B=9, 3Y=10, 4A=11, 4B=12, 4Y=13.

   Effect, with the real part fitted:
   - Pin 10 (3Y) drives MPU0's device bus ~{WD} (J1 pin 11), and pin 13 (4Y) drives its A3 (J1 pin 8).  Both are
     shared by every device on MPU0's bus.
   - Pins 1 and 4 (1Y, 2Y) drive ~Y1 and ~Y2 against IC4.
   - Pins 3, 6 and 8 are inputs where the gate outputs should be, so IC13, IC12 and IC14's clocks float, and those
     registers load at random: the other MPUs get random resets, NMIs and IRQs.
   - Pin 11 (4A) is an input on ~A3, so IC4's E3 floats and the decode is random.

   Fix: correct the symbol's pin numbers (it is also used by Device Decode stage A and B, Kernal Mode, Device Reset,
   Instruction Code and MPU NMI) and re-route IC10.  Better, see fault 2.

2. The registers latch at the start of the write, before the data is valid (IC12, IC13, IC14)

   Yn.WD = NOR(~Yn, ~WD) rises when ~{WD} falls, at the start of the write, and the '273s load on that rising edge.
   ~{WD} falls as PHI2 rises, and the W65C816's write data is only valid tMDS after that.

   Effect: the registers load whatever was on the data bus before the write, usually the bank byte.

   Fix: clock them from OR(~Yn, ~WD), which rises at the end of the write: a 74LVC32 in IC10's place does all three,
   and also avoids the faulty symbol.  ~A3 then needs an inverter of its own (for example a 74LVC1G04), as IC1, IC2
   and IC15 have no spare gates.  Check the data hold time against the delay of ~{WD} after PHI2 falls: the W65C816
   holds write data for 10ns.

3. IC16 drives the data bus on every device read, not only this board's (IC16)

   IC16's ~OE1 and ~OE2 are both ~{RD}.  On the Mainboard Full that is MPU0's D~{RD}, the device bus read strobe that
   every device on MPU0's bus shares.

   Effect: on every device read by MPU0, from any device, IC16 drives D0-D7 against the device being read: it puts 0
   on D3-D7, and on D0-D2 whatever DO0-DO2 float to.  DO0-DO2 have no driver unless one of IC7, IC8, IC9 or IC11 is
   enabled.

   Fix: enable IC16 only for this board's own registers, for example ~OE1 = AND(~Y0, ~Y1, ~Y2, ~Y3) (low when one of
   Y0-Y3 is selected) with ~OE2 = ~{RD}.

4. Possible: the IRQ inputs float when the other MPUs' boards are absent (IC5, IC9)

   ~{MPU1 IRQ}_{in} and ~{MPU2 IRQ}_{in} come from the Processor Small Device boards (B14, B15 on the Mainboard
   Full).  There are no pull-ups on either board or the mainboard.  With a Processor Small Device absent, its input
   floats into IC5, so ~{Device Interrupt} (device 7's interrupt to MPU0) goes on and off at random.

   Fix: pull-ups to 3.3V on ~{MPU1 IRQ}_{in} and ~{MPU2 IRQ}_{in}, on this board or the mainboard.

5. Note: this board doesn't drive the hold count

   The J1 connector has no HD0-HD2 pins, so on an access to this device MPU0's hold count bus is undriven.  See
   Processor/Processor Board/Processor Board Faults.txt, fault 5, and Component Boards/Data and Signal Buffer/Data and
   Signal Buffer Faults.txt, fault 2.
