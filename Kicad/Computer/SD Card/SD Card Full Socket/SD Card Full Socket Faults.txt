SD Card Full Socket - schematic faults
======================================

From the symbol library review of 2026-11-23.

1. The SD socket's symbol is one contact off, so power and ground are on the wrong contacts

   The symbol JST:SDK-9BNS-K13-GS-TB_LF__SN_1 names its pins 1=DAT2, 2=CD/DAT3, 3=CMD, 4=VSS, 5=VDD, 6=CLK, 7=VSS,
   8=DAT0, 9=DAT1.  JST numbers the socket's circuits the same as the SD card's pins: 1=CD/DAT3, 2=CMD, 3=VSS1, 4=VDD,
   5=CLK, 6=VSS2, 7=DAT0, 8=DAT1, 9=DAT2.  Its drawing has No.9 beside No.1, and the footprint (SamacSys_Parts:
   SDK9BNSK13GSTBLFSN1) follows it: pad 9 is at the end beside pad 1, and pads 7 and 8 are on the narrow pitch.

   Effect: on the routed PCB, every signal and supply is one contact along:
   - VDD (pad 4) is on GND.
   - CLK (pad 5) is on 3.3V.
   - VSS1 (pad 3) and VSS2 (pad 6) carry signals (CMD and CLK).
   - DAT0-DAT2 and CD/DAT3 are each on the neighbouring contact.
   A card fitted to this board has its supply pin grounded while 3.3V and the signals drive its clock and ground
   pins.  It won't work, and the 3.3V supply is shorted through the card to GND.  The card detect (CD1),
   write protect (WP1) and shell pins are right.

   Fix: renumber the symbol's pins to the SD numbering (see Symbols/Symbol Faults.txt, item 2), update the symbol in
   this schematic, and re-route the board.
