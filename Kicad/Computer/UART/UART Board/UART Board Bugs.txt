HCP65 UART Board (V0) - schematic bugs
======================================

1. Device ROM and device RAM selects swapped at the Stage A decoder (B4)

   B4 (Device Decode Stage A) pin 23, ~{Device_RAM}, is on the net ~{Device ROM},
   which goes to IC3 (SST39VF010, the device ROM) pin 22 ~{CE} and to B2
   (Clock Hold Standard) pin 5 ~{Device_ROM}.

   B4 pin 25, ~{Device_ROM}, is on the net ~{Device RAM}, which goes to B2 pin 2
   ~{Device_RAM}.

   The Audio Board and the SD Card Board wire the same symbol the other way:
   Stage A pin 25 (~{Device_ROM}) drives the ROM's ~{CE} and pin 23 (~{Device_RAM})
   drives ~{Device RAM}.

   Effect: the device ROM is selected by the device RAM range instead of the ROM
   range, so the kernal's device scan won't find the board's description at the
   start of the slot. The Clock Hold board also gets each select on the wrong
   input.

   Fix: swap the two net labels at B4 pins 23 and 25.
