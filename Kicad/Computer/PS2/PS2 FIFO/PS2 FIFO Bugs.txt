HCP65 PS2 FIFO (V0) - schematic bugs
====================================

1. The FIFOs are held in reset whenever the computer runs

   IC2 and IC6 (CD74HCT40105M) have pin 9, MR, on the net ~{Reset} (J1.9).  The
   40105's MR is active high: "A high on the MASTER RESET (MR) sets all the
   control logic marker bits to 0.  DOR goes low and DIR goes high."  ~{Reset}
   is high whenever the board is out of reset, so the FIFOs are always being
   reset: they never hold a byte and Output Ready (IC6 DOR) never goes high.

   The shift registers are right: IC5 and IC9 (74LVC595) have pin 10 ~{MR},
   which is active low, on the same net.

   Fix: put pin 9 of IC2 and IC6 on Reset, ~{Reset} inverted.  IC4 (74LVC04) has
   a spare inverter, 6A (pin 13) to 6Y (pin 12): ~{Reset} to 6A, 6Y to both MRs.

2. The byte is latched, and put into the FIFO, at the start of the next frame

   Packet Pulse is high while a frame is coming in: the first fall of the PS/2
   clock charges the PS2 Timing board's C3 through D1, and it only falls once
   the clock has been high for about 0.6ms (R3 4.7K, C3 0.1uF).  It is on IC5
   and IC9's STCP (pin 12), and Packet Pulse_{2} (two inverters later) on IC2
   and IC6's SI (pin 3).  STCP and SI both act on a rising edge, so both happen
   at the start of a frame, not its end: just after that frame's first clock has
   shifted its start bit in.  So each byte is latched a frame late (the last
   byte of a code waits for the next key) and a bit out of place: the outputs'
   wiring (/Q0 on IC5 Q1 ... /Q7 on IC9 Q2) is for a latch after the eleventh
   bit.

   Packet Waiting (IC3's 2CP on ~{Packet Pulse}) is set at the end of the
   frame, as it should be, so a driver woken by it would read the byte before.

   Effect: with bug 1 fixed, reading the FIFO gives the previous frame's bits
   shifted by one: d1 to d7 and parity in place of d0 to d7.

   Fix: latch and load on the fall of Packet Pulse.  ~{Packet Pulse} (J1.2) is
   already on the sheet for STCP.  SI needs the inverse of Packet Pulse_{2}, so
   that it still comes after STCP: the PS2 Timing board's ~{Packet Pulse}_{2}
   (IC4 5Y, pin 10) can be taken out through a spare connector pin (J2 pins 2,
   3, 4, 9, 11, 13 to 16, 24 to 27 and 29 to 32 are unconnected) to the FIFO's
   SI pins in place of Packet Pulse_{2}.

The emulator (HCP65Lib's CHCP65PS2Port) works as the board is meant to: the
FIFO is only reset with the board, and a byte goes in when its packet ends.

A thing to check on the real board, not a bug: a packet only ends after about
0.6ms of the clock high (0.44ms to 0.95ms over the 74VHCT14's VN of 0.5V to
1.5V).  Frames a device sends closer together than that run into one packet,
and only the last byte of them is kept.  The emulator's far end leaves 1ms
between frames.
