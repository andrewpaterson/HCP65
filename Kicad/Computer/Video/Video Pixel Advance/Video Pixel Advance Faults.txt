HCP65 Video Pixel Advance - schematic faults
============================================

1. CEP has no pull

   IC6 (74LVC163) pin 7 CEP only reaches J1.32, and the Video Board - Signals
   leaves B1 pin 32 unconnected, so it floats (Video Board - Signals Faults.txt
   fault 4).  A pull-up here, or tying it to 3.3V on the main board, fixes it.


2. The pattern isn't started at the start of a line

   IC6 counts 4 to 15 (a 12 step pattern) and is only loaded by its own TC or
   ~{Reset} (IC3, 74LVC2G08: ~{PE} = ~{TC} AND ~{Reset}).  Enable Pattern
   (~{Horizontal Blank}) only gates the output.

   Effect: the pattern runs on through blanking, so each line starts at a
   different step of it: (line length mod 12) further on each line.  A
   640x480 line is 800 clocks, 8 steps on.  Patterns that repeat pixels
   (every 2nd, 3rd, 6th or 12th) start in a different place on each line, so
   the picture jags.  Patterns of all 1s are unaffected.

   Fix: load the counter during horizontal blank: put ~{Horizontal Blank}
   (Enable Pattern) into IC3's gate in place of, or ANDed with, ~{Reset}.


3. The two muxes overlap as they change over

   IC1 and IC5 (PI3B3251) are enabled by Q3 and ~{Q3}, and their
   disable time (up to 5.5ns) is longer than their enable time (up to
   3.5ns), so at steps 7 to 8 and 15 to 4 both are on for a moment and short
   two of the pattern latch's outputs together.  Harmless if it settles
   before Advance Pixel is sampled.
