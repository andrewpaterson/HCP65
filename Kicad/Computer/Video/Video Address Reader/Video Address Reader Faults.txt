HCP65 Video Address Reader - schematic faults
=============================================

1. Reading the pixel address keeps reloading what is being read

   The three 74LVC574s are clocked by the pixel clock ANDed with Read Address
   Low, and Read Address Low also enables the low byte's outputs.  So for the
   whole of a read of the low byte ($402) the latches take the counter again
   on every pixel clock while the low byte drives the bus.

   Effect: the processor can take the low byte while its bits are changing,
   and the high and bank bytes ($403, $404), read after, are from the last
   pixel clock of the low byte's read, which may not be the address the
   processor got in the low byte.  (Reading the low byte first is needed for
   any snapshot at all.)

   Fix: take the address once per read, for instance clock the latches from
   a flip-flop that synchronises Read Address Low to the pixel clock.  The
   Video Counter 12bit's read latches have the same fault.
