HCP65 Video Memory 2MB - schematic faults
=========================================

1. Two drivers on ~CE, ~OE and ~WE as ~E changes (low severity)

   The 74LVC257 that selects the SRAM's ~CE, ~OE and ~WE and the 74LVC126 that
   holds them high (deselected) share the same enable (D~OE, from ~E through
   the 74LVC2G34) with opposite polarity: the 257's outputs are enabled when
   it is low, the 126's when it is high.  As ~E changes, one turns on before
   the other is off, and for a moment both drive the three lines, against
   each other if their levels differ.

   Effect: a brief fight, and a glitch on the SRAM's controls, at every
   change of ~E: as the CPU's accesses to the frame it has begin and end, and
   at every Memory Frame change.

   Fix: pull-ups on ~CE, ~OE and ~WE in place of the 74LVC126 (the 257
   tri-stated leaves them high), or enable the two from separate, non
   overlapping signals.
