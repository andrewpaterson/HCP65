HCP65 Audio Board - schematic faults
====================================

1. The Flags & Interrupt's ~{Reset} (B8 pin 32) isn't connected

   B8 is a Flags & Interrupt, whose pin 32 is ~{Reset}: it is what clears
   Enable.  The HCP65:Flags_Interrupt symbol names the pin N.C., so it is
   left unconnected here (the netlist's unconnected-(B8-N.C.-Pad32)), and
   the Flags board's input floats.

   Effect: reset doesn't turn the board's Enable off, and Enable can come
   up on or change by itself.

   Fix: once the symbol's pin 32 is ~{Reset}, wire it to ~{Reset} (Device
   Decode Stage A's ~{Reset}_OUT).  See Component Boards/Flags Enable
   Interrupt/Flags Enable Interrupt Faults.txt.

The board also uses standard boards with faults of their own, which it has
with them (see each one's Faults file in Component Boards):
  - Clock Hold Standard: a NOR where an OR is needed, so the clock hold code
    is fought over on every cycle.


Found in the socket review of 2026-11-23
----------------------------------------

2. The sound RAMs' top address lines are shifted by one (B5, Selector 12bit)

   On a Selector 12bit, output Qn passes input An or Bn.  B5's Q1, Q2 and Q3 drive M12, M13 and M14, so their inputs
   must be A12/C12, A13/C13 and A14/C14.  The board feeds them one pin along: GND on pins 11 and 24 (Q3's inputs), A14
   and C14 on pins 12 and 25 (Q2), A13 and C13 on 13 and 26 (Q1), and A12 and C12 on 14 and 27 (Q0, which goes
   nowhere).

   Effect: M14 is always 0, M13 carries A14/C14 and M12 carries A13/C13, and bit 12 is lost.  Only the lower 16KB of
   each 32KB sound RAM (B4, B6) is used; processor addresses that differ only in A12 land on the same byte, and
   playback reads each 4KB block twice.

   Fix: move the inputs down one pin (A14/C14 on pins 11/24, A13/C13 on 12/25, A12/C12 on 13/26, GND on 14/27), or take
   M12-M14 from Q0-Q2.

3. The Audio Address Decode gets CLK where it needs ~CLK (B12 pin 10)

   B12 pin 10 is ~{CLK} on the Audio Address Decode board (and in this board's own symbol), but it is wired to /CLK.
   /~{CLK} exists on the board (B13 pin 26).  On the daughter, Sound Address = pin 10 AND ~Device RAM drives the LE of
   both sound output latches (B4/B6 IC3, 74LVC573).  The sound RAMs drive DM while CLK is low (~OE = CLK, from B5), the
   counter steps on CLK rising, and the DAC writes happen while CLK is low.  So the latches must be open while CLK is
   low and close when it rises: LE = ~CLK.

   Effect: with CLK, the latches are open while the RAMs are off and DM floats, and close as the RAM starts to drive.
   The DAC gets the floating bus, at best the previous sample held by stray capacitance.

   Fix: wire B12 pin 10 to /~{CLK}.  After that, the latch closes about two gate delays after the RAM's ~OE rises and
   the address changes; that relies on the RAM's output hold or bus capacitance, so it is worth checking.
