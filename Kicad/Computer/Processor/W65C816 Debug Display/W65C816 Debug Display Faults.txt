W65C816 Debug Display - schematic faults
========================================

From the review of the Processor Board's daughter boards, 2026-11-23.

What the board does: 12V comes in on J2 to IC2 (LD1117S50, 5V).  Eight ATF16V8BQL GALs (IC5/13 data, IC6/14 A0-7,
IC7/15 A8-15, IC8/16 A16-23) decode hex for four KCDC03-123 dual common-cathode displays through 220 ohm arrays.
Their cathodes go to IC3 (BV1LB150 low-side switch), switched from ~{LED Enable} (S2-1) through IC12.  Two ABT245s
(IC10, IC11) drive 15 discrete LEDs from the control signals; an LED is lit when its signal is high.

The Processor Board footprint (B13, 49 pads) lines up with this board's headers: J4 is D7-D0 on pads 1-8, J2 12V on
9, J3 A23-A0 on 10-33, J1 GND on 34, and J5 PHI2 ... X on 35-49.

1. The BE and RDY inputs float (IC11 pin 13, IC10 pin 13)

   On the Processor Board, B13 pins 36 (BE) and 43 (RDY) are nets with nothing else on them.  The processor's BE
   and RDY are on the 3.3V net, and the "BE" and "RDY" labels near B1 don't join it (KiCad's ERC reports both).  The
   SN74ABT245B has no bus hold.

   Effect: LED2 (BE) and LED10 (RDY) flicker or glow at random.  The floating input stages can oscillate and couple
   noise into the neighbouring outputs.  There is no harm to the processor, as these outputs only drive LEDs.

   Fix: on the Processor Board, put B13 pins 36 and 43 on the same nets as B1 pins 20 and 21.  If RDY stops being
   tied to 3.3V (W65C816 Breakout Faults.txt, fault 1), connect B13 pin 43 to the processor's RDY instead.

2. The "Always Show Data" switch works the wrong way round (S2-2)

   ~{Data LED Enable} = ~{LED Enable} OR (Always Show Data AND ~{PHI2}), where Always Show Data means S2-2 closed.

   Effect: with S2-2 closed the data digits blank while PHI2 is low, and with it open they show all the time.

   Fix: invert the switch's sense (IC9/IC12), or relabel it.

3. Possible: IC2 (LD1117, SOT-223) runs too hot

   The load is up to 56 segments at roughly 5-9mA each, plus eight GALs, two ABT245s and 15 LEDs: about 0.3-0.5A at
   a 7V drop, which is 2-3.5W.

   Effect: thermal shutdown and cycling, which browns out the GALs.

   Fix: a 12V to 5V buck regulator, or 5V from the Processor Board, and larger segment resistors (about 680 ohm to
   1k).  The GALs are also asked to source well beyond their rated IOH of a few mA, so the segment brightness will
   vary; larger resistors help that too.

4. Possible: GAL power-down on pin 4

   Pin 4 (IN3/PD) of each GAL carries Data2 or Addr2/6/10/14/18/22.  If the power-down option is set in a GAL's JEDEC
   file, those digits blank.  Make sure power-down is off when programming them.
