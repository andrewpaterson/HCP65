W65C816 Power - schematic faults
================================

From the review of the Processor Board's daughter boards, 2026-11-23.

What the board does: IC1 (LK112M50) makes W65C816V = 5.0V from 12V, enabled by the Processor Board's 5V.  That rail
powers the whole Breakout, including the Breakout's own AP7365 3.3V regulator, so the 74ALVC164245's VCC(B) >= VCC(A)
rule is met.  The unconnected bypass pin is optional.

Nothing here is a definite fault; both items are worth measuring.

1. Possible: IC1 runs hot

   IC1 drops 7V in a SOT23-5 with RthJA about 255 C/W.  The load is the W65C816 (about 2mA/MHz), 16 address lines
   driven at 5V, and the Breakout's whole 3.3V side (the data bus, and ~{RD}/~{WD} to about ten boards).  At 30-50mA
   that is 0.2-0.35W, a rise of 55-90 C, close to the limit.

   Fix: drop some of the voltage in a series resistor first, or use a 5V LDO in SOT-89 or SOT-223, or a switcher.

2. Possible: reset can end before the processor has power

   W65C816V only comes up once both 12V and 5V are present.  If 12V is late, ~{Reset} could release before the
   W65C816 has had a valid VDD and two clocks.  Check this against the reset timing from the mainboard.
