HCP65 MPU Address Decode - schematic faults
===========================================

From the review of the Processor Board's daughter boards, 2026-11-23.

What the board does: IC11 ('138) decodes A19-A21 into 512KB blocks when A23 and A22 are 0 and the latched Kernal
Mode OUT is high: RAM, ROM, Device Group 32K and Device Group 128K.  IC16 splits $100000-$13FFFF into eight 32KB
slots (~{Registers}, then ~{Device}1-7), and IC33 splits $180000-$1FFFFF into ~{Device}16-19; both are gated by
~{Reset}.  IC38 and IC39 decode A0-A3 into Register0-15.  ~{Main} is low except below $200000 in kernel mode.
~{Devices} is either device group except the registers slot.  IC3 ('125) swaps ~{RAM} and ~{ROM} while RAM Swap (the
Native Latch) is low.  This all matches memory-map.md, and the polarities are right.

Neither item is a definite fault.

1. Possible: IC3's swap buffers overlap when RAM Swap changes

   Buffers 1 and 2 are enabled through the IC1 inverter, buffers 3 and 4 by RAM Swap directly.  For one inverter
   delay, IC3 pins 3 and 11 both drive ~{RAM}_I, and pins 6 and 8 both drive ~{ROM}_I, at opposite levels if the
   access is to RAM or ROM.

   Effect: a short fight once per boot, when the Native Latch sets.

   Fix: accept it, or make sure the XCE that sets the latch isn't directly followed by a RAM or ROM access.

2. Possible: ~{Devices} isn't gated by reset

   IC11 isn't gated by ~{Reset}, but IC16 and IC33 are.  During reset, ~{Devices} can go low for $100000-$1FFFFF
   accesses while no single device select does.

   Fix: gate IC11's device group outputs with ~{Reset} as well, if any board relies on ~{Devices} during reset.
