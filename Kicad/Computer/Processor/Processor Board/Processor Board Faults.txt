Processor Board - schematic faults
==================================

From the review of the Processor Board's daughter boards, 2026-11-23.  These are faults in the Processor Board's own
wiring between the sockets.  Each daughter board's own faults are in a "... Faults.txt" next to its schematic.

Every socket's pins (B1-B23) were matched against the daughter board's connector, and J5 and J1 against the
Mainboard Small and Mainboard Full MPU slots.  J5's and J1's pin names all match the mainboard.  Every address and
data bus reaches the mainboard in the right bit order, even where a daughter board's labels are reversed.

1. Main memory ~RD and ~WD are swapped (B7 pins 60 and 61)

   B7 pin 60 is the Main Memory Address board's ~{Main WD} output, but it goes to J5.D10, which is the mainboard's
   ~{Main RD} (the SRAM's ~OE).  Pin 61 (~{Main RD}) goes to J5.D11, ~{Main WD}.  Every main memory read becomes a
   write.  See Main Memory Address Faults.txt, fault 1.

   Fix: swap the nets on B7 pins 60 and 61.

2. RDY is tied to 3.3V on a 5V processor (B1 pin 21)

   The W65C816 runs at 5V, and RDY's input-high threshold is 4.0V.  WAI drives RDY low straight into the 3.3V
   plane.  See W65C816 Breakout Faults.txt, fault 1.

   Fix: leave B1 pin 21 unconnected (the Breakout's R1 pulls it up to 5V).  BE (B1 pin 20) is fine at 3.3V.

3. The Debug Display's BE and RDY inputs float (B13 pins 36 and 43)

   The nets /BE and /RDY have only B13 on them.  The "BE" and "RDY" labels near B1 aren't on the 3.3V wire, so they
   don't join it.  KiCad's ERC reports both.

   Fix: put B13 pins 36 and 43 on the same nets as B1 pins 20 and 21.

4. The hold count bits are reversed (B4 pins 44-46, B8 pins 37-39)

   MPU Memory's H0 (B4 pin 44) and the main bus's HD0 (J5.A31, through B8 pin 39) are on net /H2, which is Hold
   Count's Latch2; H2 and HD2 reach Latch0.  See Hold Count Faults.txt, fault 2.

   H0 is the least significant bit, as the MPU Memory board and the main bus have it, so this is the Processor
   Board's fault.  Fix: swap B23 pins 5 and 7 (Hold Count's Latch0 and Latch2).  /H0-/H2 only join B4, B8 and B23,
   so this one swap fixes both sources.

5. NMI and hold inputs from the mainboard have no pull-ups

   ~{NMI}_{1} and ~{NMI}_{2} (J5.B4, B5), MPU0 and MPU1 (J5.B8, B7) and HD0-HD2 (J5.A29-A31) all float when nothing
   on the mainboard drives them.  The floating NMI inputs give spurious NMIs (MPU NMI Faults.txt, fault 3), and
   HD0-HD2 give random hold counts (Data and Signal Buffer Faults.txt, fault 2).

   Fix: pull-ups to 3.3V, like R6 and R7.

6. Minor: B10 pin 24 (GND) has no connection

   The MPU Timer board has GND on that pin.  Its other three grounds are connected.

7. Minor: the Hold Count board's presence pin isn't read (B23 pin 12)

   B20 pin 28 (S11) is free, tied only to GND.  Wiring B23 pin 12 to B20 pin 28 in its place gives Hold Count a
   presence bit.

8. Note: header J2 carries the push-pull ~{NMI} and ~{IRQ} outputs (pins 5 and 2)

   A button or open-drain source on J2 would fight the MPU NMI and Device Interrupts boards' outputs.  Treat J2 as
   monitor only.

9. Possible: IC3 (LD1117, 12V to 5V) dissipation

   IC3 supplies every daughter board's 5V, and through their own regulators their 3.3V, with a 7V drop in a SOT-223.
   Estimate the total current: at 300mA it is already 2.1W.


Found comparing the Processor Board with Mainboard Small, 2026-11-23
--------------------------------------------------------------------

10. On Mainboard Small the processor's main memory slot doesn't line up with PHI2

   The board's PHI2 is the inverse of MPU CLK (J5.B3), but the mainboard's ~Main Slot (J5.B11) is low only while MPU
   CLK is high, so it closes as PHI2 rises.  The processor can't reach main memory.  See Main/Triple MPU Clock Generator
   Faults.txt.  MPU CLK there is also 33MHz, from a 100MHz oscillator.

11. ~ABORT is pulled to 5V by the mainboard (J5.B6)

   Mainboard Small's R4 pulls ~ABORT up to 5V, and it lands on the Breakout's 3.3V 74ALVC164245 input.  See Mainboard
   Small Faults.txt, fault 3.
