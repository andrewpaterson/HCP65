W65C816 Minimal - schematic faults
==================================

From the review of 2026-11-23.  Only the data transceiver was checked; the rest of the board hasn't been reviewed.

1. The data transceiver is the wrong way round (IC6, 74LVC4245)

   The 74LVC4245's A port runs at VCCA (pin 1, 5V) and its B port at VCCB (pins 23-24, 3.3V).  Here the A port (A0-A7,
   pins 3-10) is the connector's D0-D7 and the B port (B0-B7, pins 21-14) is the W65C816's own data pins (/D0_65 ..
   /D7_65).  The W65C816 (IC2) runs from 5V (VDD on the 5V net).

   Effect:
   - On reads the B port drives the processor's data pins with 3.3V highs, below the W65C816's 4.0V input high at 5V,
     so read data is unreliable.
   - On writes the processor drives 5V into the B port, above its VCCB + 0.5V maximum.
   - The A port drives the connector at 5V.  On the coprocessor boards that is the coprocessor's memory bus, with
     IS61WV5128 3.3V SRAMs that aren't 5V tolerant.
   The W65C816 Breakout has it the right way round: the processor on the 5V side.

   Fix: swap the ports, so the processor's D0-D7 are on A0-A7 (5V) and the connector's on B0-B7 (3.3V), and check DIR:
   with the ports swapped, R/~W high (a read) must drive B to A.

Used on Coprocessor Board - Main (B2, B6) and Coprocessor Board - Additional (B18, B24).  Those boards' own faults
files cover how the sockets are wired (PHI2 into the Latch boards, and ~DE).
