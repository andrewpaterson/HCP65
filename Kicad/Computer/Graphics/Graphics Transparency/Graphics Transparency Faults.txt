Graphics Transparency - schematic faults
========================================

From the socket review of 2026-11-23.  The board's own circuit hasn't been reviewed beyond this.

1. Possible: the holding latch closes at the start of a read (IC4)

   IC4 (74LVC573) is a holding latch on MD, with its LE on pin 2.  The Graphics Board (B20) and Blitter Board (B2) both
   feed pin 2 with ~Main Read.  LE is then high, and the latch transparent, whenever no read is happening, and it
   closes at the start of the next read, before MD carries the new byte.  Its D and Q are both on MD and its outputs
   are enabled by ~Write, so in a write it puts the floating MD bus back onto itself.

   Effect: the byte held for the write survives only on bus charge and a three-gate delay.

   Fix: LE needs to be high during the read and fall at its end, which is Main Read with active-high polarity: invert
   it here or on the hosts.  See Graphics Board Faults.txt, fault 14.
