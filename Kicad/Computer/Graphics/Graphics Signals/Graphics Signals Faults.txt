Graphics Signals - schematic faults
===================================

From the socket review of 2026-11-23.  The board's own circuit hasn't been reviewed.

1. ~Done is on a pin the socket symbol doesn't connect (pin 9)

   On this board ~{Done} is on pin 9, into IC2's ~1RD and ~2RD (pins 1 and 13).  The HCP65:Graphics_Signals symbol has
   ~Done on pin 4, so host boards wire ~Done there and leave pin 9 open.

   Effect: on the Graphics Board and Blitter Board (B19) IC2's resets float, so the flip-flops clear at random.  See
   Graphics Board Faults.txt and Blitter Board Faults.txt, fault 2.

   Fix: put ~Done on pin 9 in the symbol and update both host boards.

Checked and not a fault: pin 31 (~MCLK) is driven on this board (IC9's ~Y through R2) and only used here, so the hosts
leaving it open is harmless.
