GPIO Controller Board - schematic faults
========================================

From the socket review of 2026-11-23: every socket's pins were compared with its daughter board.  The rest of the
board hasn't been reviewed.

1. The Pico's RUN pin is driven by the 5V reset (B7, Power and Reset, pin 29)

   The HCP65_Power_and_Reset socket symbol names pin 29 ~RES_3V and pin 32 ~RES_5V, but the Power and Reset board has
   them the other way round: pin 29 (J3.9) is ~Reset_5V, from IC4 (74LVC1G17 on 5V), and pin 32 (J3.12) is
   ~Reset_3.3V, from IC7 (74LVC1G17 on 3.3V).  This board takes its reset from pin 29 and leaves pin 32 unconnected.

   Effect: a push-pull 5V reset drives /~{Reset}, which goes to the Pico's RUN pin (J6.11, Pico pin 30).  The RP2040
   isn't 5V tolerant, so this can damage the Pico.

   Fix: swap the two pin names in the Power_and_Reset symbol, update it here, and take /~{Reset} from pin 32.
