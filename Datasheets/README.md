# Datasheets

The makers' datasheets of the parts the HCP65's boards use: the reference a schematic's symbols and footprints are
checked against (`Kicad/Symbols/Symbol Faults.txt` was made from them).  They aren't part of the website.

| Files | Are | From |
|---|---|---|
| `*.pdf` | Third-party | Each part's maker, or a distributor, downloaded as the maker publishes it; the PDF names its maker and its revision. |
